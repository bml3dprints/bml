Founded in 2025

BML 3D Prints began as a passion project by Brenda Shen, Maxi Zhang, and Lydia Kurniawan during 7th grade. Inspired by our Technology Education teacher, Mrs. Amy Schroeder, we set out to explore the creative possibilities of 3D printing.

Mission

Our mission is to spark creativity and innovation through 3D printing. We provide resources and inspiration for students, hobbyists, and makers of all ages—helping ideas become reality, one layer at a time.
